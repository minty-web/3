04

Studio

We draw slowly and build carefully. Most of our buildings are smaller than they could have been, and they’re better for it.

  1. I

    Light first

    In the north, light is the scarcest material we have. Every plan starts from where the low sun will be in February.

  2. II

    Build less

    Fewer square metres, better made. The greenest building is the one sized to its life, not to its budget.

  3. III

    Belong to the ground

    Timber from local forests, stone from the site, and foundations that let the snow drift through.
